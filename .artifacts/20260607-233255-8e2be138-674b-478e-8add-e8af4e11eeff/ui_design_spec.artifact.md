# Ntanda LMS Mobile App - UI Design Specifications

This document defines the visual and functional requirements for the core LMS-focused screens, incorporating the "Premium" design style requested.

## General Design System
- **Theme**: White and Light Blue (Professional, Educational).
- **Typography**: Clean, modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Roboto).
- **Vibe**: Trustworthy, efficient, "Advanced".

---

## Screen 3: Onboarding 2 - Offline Learning
- **Visuals**: A student studying on a tablet/smartphone in a remote location (e.g., a park or commute) without Wi-Fi, showing a "Downloaded" status.
- **Title**: "Learn Anywhere, Anytime"
- **Subtitle**: "Download your course modules and study offline without worrying about data or connectivity."
- **Functional Elements**: Progress indicators, "Download" icon animations.

## Screen 4: Onboarding 3 - AI-Powered Success
- **Visuals**: A student interacting with a friendly AI avatar or a clean chat interface that highlights key course concepts.
- **Title**: "Your Personal AI Tutor"
- **Subtitle**: "Get instant answers to your questions and personalized study recommendations directly within your lessons."
- **Functional Elements**: Floating chat bubble visualization, glowing "AI" accents.

## Screen 5: Login Screen
- **Header**: Educational/Institutional themed illustration at the top (e.g., a modern library or a student with a laptop).
- **Theme**: White background with light blue accents.
- **Fields**:
    - Phone Number or Email (Toggle/Dual support).
    - Password.
- **Buttons**:
    - "Sign In" (Primary Blue).
    - "Forgot Password" link.
- **Aesthetic**: Minimalist, clean educational UI.

## Screen 6: Registration Screen
- **Header**: Ntanda Institutional Branding/Logo.
- **Fields**:
    - Full Name
    - Phone Number
    - Email Address
    - Password (with strength indicator)
- **Buttons**: Large "Join Ntanda" button.
- **Theme**: White and Light Blue, professional academic design.
